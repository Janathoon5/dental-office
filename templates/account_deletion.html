This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated data from the Dental Office Patient Portal app and website.

How to Request Deletion

Email {{ contact_email }} with the subject line "Account Deletion Request." Please include your full name and date of birth so we can verify and locate your record. We will confirm your request and deactivate your portal and app login access within 5 business days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your login credentials and app/portal account access.
  • Device push-notification registrations.
  • Any data that is not part of your clinical or billing record.

What May Be Retained

As a dental care provider, we are required by law to retain certain health, treatment, and billing records for a minimum period even after an account deletion request, in accordance with standard medical recordkeeping requirements. This may include treatment records, treatment plans, X-rays and clinical photos, invoices, and payment history. This retained data is encrypted at rest and is not accessible through the app or website once your account access has been deactivated — it remains subject to the same access controls and audit logging described in our Privacy Policy.
