How Recalls Work

A patient's next cleaning is due a set number of months after their last completed cleaning or checkup. The default is 6 months; you can change it on each patient's edit page.

Patients who already have a cleaning or checkup booked are left off this list.

Recall emails also go out automatically each morning (see below).
