This Privacy Policy describes how our dental practice ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information through our patient portal website and mobile app (together, the "Services").

Information We Collect

  • Contact and identity information: name, date of birth, phone number, email, address.
  • Insurance information.
  • Health and treatment information: treatment records, treatment plans, medical alerts and notes, X-rays, and photos you or our staff upload.
  • Billing information: invoices and payment records.
  • Messages exchanged between you and our staff through the Services.
  • Basic account and device information needed to sign you in and, on the mobile app, to deliver push notifications.

How We Use Information

We use this information solely to provide dental care coordination for you as a patient of our practice: scheduling appointments, maintaining your treatment history, billing, and communicating with you about your care. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

How We Protect Information

  • Sensitive health information is encrypted in our database.
  • All connections to our Services are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Our mobile app additionally pins the expected server certificate to guard against network-level interception.
  • The mobile app supports biometric app-lock and blocks screenshots of sensitive screens.
  • X-rays and photos are stored in encrypted cloud storage and are only ever accessed through your authenticated account.
  • We keep an audit trail of who accesses and changes health records.

Third-Party Services

We use a small number of third-party services to operate the Services:

  • Hosting (Railway) — hosts our website and database.
  • Cloud storage (Amazon S3) — stores X-ray and photo images.
  • Email delivery (Resend) — sends account and appointment emails.
  • Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — delivers notification alerts to the mobile app. Notification content is always a generic message (e.g. "You have a new message from your dental office") — the actual content of your health information is never included in a push notification and never passes through this service.

Your Choices and Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting our office directly using the information below. Health records are retained in accordance with applicable recordkeeping requirements even where deletion is requested, consistent with standard medical recordkeeping practice.

Children's Privacy

The Services are intended for use by patients and, where applicable, a parent or guardian managing a minor patient's care. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children outside of that context.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your information, please contact our office directly at the practice's phone number or address on file.
